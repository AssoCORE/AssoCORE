## Why AssoCORE?

AssoCORE was born from real field experience working with associations. We understand the frustration of juggling multiple disparate tools—Excel spreadsheets, scattered Google Drive folders, endless email threads. Traditional association management software is often heavy, complex, and unintuitive.

We're building something different: a modern, user-centric platform that puts **UI/UX at the heart of development**. Our goal is to make association management feel effortless, not overwhelming.
