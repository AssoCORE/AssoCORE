Practical, step-by-step guides for specific tasks and workflows in AssoCORE.

## DevOps & Deployment

### [Docker Basics](./docker-basics/)

Complete beginner's guide to Docker concepts and commands. Learn about containers, images, docker-compose, and how everything works together.

**Perfect for:** Team members who are new to Docker and containerization.

---

### [Kubernetes Deployment](./kubernetes-deployment/)

Deploy AssoCORE on Kubernetes using k3d. Includes complete kubectl reference and troubleshooting guide.

**Perfect for:** Getting your first Kubernetes cluster running and understanding the basics.

---

### [Observability & Monitoring](./observability-monitoring/)

Monitor your AssoCORE deployment with Prometheus and Grafana. Set up dashboards, alerts, and understand what's happening in your cluster.

**Perfect for:** Understanding cluster health, troubleshooting performance issues, and setting up production monitoring.
