## What is AssoCORE?

AssoCORE is a modern, user-centric platform designed to simplify association management. Born from real-world experience working with associations, we understand the frustration of juggling multiple disparate tools—endless Excel spreadsheets, scattered Google Drive folders, and chaotic email threads.

## The Problem We're Solving

Most associations struggle with:

- **Fragmented Tools**: Managing members in Excel, events in Google Calendar, finances in another tool
- **Email Overload**: Critical information lost in email threads
- **Outdated Software**: Heavy, complex, unintuitive association management systems
- **High Costs**: Expensive solutions not designed for associations
- **Poor Mobile Experience**: Tools that don't work well on smartphones
- **Limited Collaboration**: Difficulty coordinating between board members and volunteers

## Our Solution

AssoCORE brings everything together in one modern, intuitive platform:

### **Member Management**

- Centralized member database
- Automated membership renewals
- Communication history tracking
- Custom member fields

### **Event Organization**

- Event planning and registration
- Attendance tracking
- Automated reminders
- Payment integration

### **Financial Management**

- Membership fee tracking
- Donation processing
- Expense management
- Financial reporting

### **Communication**

- Targeted messaging
- Email campaigns
- Announcements
- Discussion forums

### **Collaboration**

- Document sharing
- Task management
- Board meeting tools
- Volunteer coordination

## What Makes Us Different

### **UI/UX First**

Unlike traditional association software that feels like it's from the 1990s, we put user experience at the heart of everything we do. Beautiful, intuitive interfaces that people actually enjoy using.

### **Modern Technology**

Built with cutting-edge technology (FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes) for speed, reliability, and scalability.

### **Open & Transparent**

Open source development with a clear roadmap and active community involvement.

### **Data-Driven**

Our features are validated through [field studies](../studies/field/) with real associations, not just guesswork.

### **Built by Association People**

Created by people who actually work with associations and understand their unique challenges.

### **Scalable**

Works for small community groups (10 members) to large organizations (10,000+ members).
