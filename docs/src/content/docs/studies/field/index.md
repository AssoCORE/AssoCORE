Research conducted with real associations to understand their needs, challenges, and how AssoCORE helps them succeed.

## Purpose

Field studies help us:

- Understand real-world association operations
- Validate features with actual users
- Identify pain points and opportunities
- Measure impact and outcomes

## Study Methodology

Our field studies typically include:

1. **Context Research** - Understanding the association's background
2. **Observation** - Watching how they work day-to-day
3. **Interviews** - Speaking with team members and stakeholders
4. **Data Collection** - Gathering quantitative metrics
5. **Analysis** - Identifying patterns and insights
6. **Recommendations** - Suggesting improvements

## Study Types

- **Adoption Studies** - How associations adopt and integrate AssoCORE
- **Usage Studies** - How features are actually used
- **Impact Studies** - Measuring AssoCORE's impact on operations
- **Needs Assessment** - Understanding unmet needs
