Analysis of modern frontmobile frameworks to determine the most efficient engine for our association management platform.

## Methodology

This study evaluates frameworks based on their suitability for high-concurrency member management and API-first architectures. We benchmarked:
* **Developer Velocity:** Time from setup to a working web server.
* **Ecosystem Maturity:** Availability of production-ready libraries for components.
* **Architecture:** Ease of creation for a clean and solid architecture.

## Criteria

1.  **Ease of use:** Ability to add, remove and test components easily.
2.  **Type Safety:** Automated validation to prevent runtime errors in member data.
3.  **Integration Ease:** How smoothly it interacts with external our backends technology.

## Findings

### Performance vs. Velocity Matrix

| Framework | Lang | Performance (TTFB) | Dev Speed | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flutter** | Dart | **Highest (300-600 ms)** | High | App that touches as many people as possible |
| **Tauri** | TypeScript | Moderate (700-1400 ms) | Moderate | Secondary apps |
| **Capacitor** | TypeScript | High (600-1200 ms) | **Highest** | Fast MVP and internal tools |

### Detailed Analysis

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Flutter (Chosen)">
    **Flutter** offers the best balance for EIP projects. It's portability and ease of use makes it a perfect fit for our needs.

  <TabItem label="Tauri">

    **Capacitor** would be good for quick development but it's lesser portability makes it less desirable.

## Recommendations

<Aside type="tip" title="Final Decision: Flutter">
  We will use **Flutter**.
</Aside>

### Strategic Justification:
* **Ease of use:** Flutter is very to use, especially for mobile apps due to its design.
* **Performance:** Its low TTFB compared to other framework will make it more aggreeable to work with and will improve the user experience.
* **Greater experience:** Our team greater experience is Flutter will make it easier for them to develop a prototype and MVP.
